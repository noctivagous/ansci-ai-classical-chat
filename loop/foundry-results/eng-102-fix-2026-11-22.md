# ENG-102 — told-location recording and reading: fix record 2026-11-22

## Design

Two coordinated halves, both narrow:

1. Recording ([turn.ts](file:///Users/jpratt/Developer/ansci-ai-classical-chat/src/ansci/turn.ts)
   record section): a copular-locative telling — non-verb root with an
   incoming `location` edge, indicative mood, no predicate edge (that
   branch keeps precedence) — is recorded as a `be` event with the
   subject as theme and the resolved location plus its qualified text
   (`New York`). Questions never record (mood gate); unresolved
   pronouns (`it`/`them`) and first/second-person subjects stay out —
   the visitor's own location has separate machinery. Bare
   existentials record nothing (no location told).
2. Reading (`answerWhereQuestion`): before entity grounding, consult
   the event ledger (latest first) for a location attached to the
   question's subject; answer `You told me the X is in <text>.` with a
   `recordAnswer` trace mirroring the visitor-location reader. The
   conversation's own record takes precedence over lexical grounding —
   that precedence is what fixes the comedienne misanswer.

Deliberately unchanged: bare existentials (no location told), the
game-level follow-up (the location was told of the ball, not the game
— still an honest decline, kept as a negative suite control), polar
confirmation of told events (broader gap, out of scope).

## Side gain (no extra code)

Verb-told locations already in the ledger now answer too: "Where is
the dog?" after "The dog sleeps in the garden" → "You told me the dog
is in garden." Previously unanswerable — the reader never consulted
events.

## Limits

`locationText` is used verbatim ("in garden"), matching the echo
reader's existing shape. Be-events are invisible to the
accumulated-tellings reader ("What did I tell you about the ball?"
still says nothing) — no garble, but no coverage either; a future
slice if wanted. "Was the ball in Boston?"-style polar confirmation
of told events remains unbuilt.
