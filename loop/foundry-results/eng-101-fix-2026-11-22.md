# ENG-101 — passive-tense representation: fix record 2026-11-22

Diagnosis (traced in `bda8a60`): representation defect. In the passive
branch, [parse.ts](file:///Users/jpratt/Developer/ansci-ai-classical-chat/src/ansci/parse.ts)
computed `tense` from the `did` auxiliary or the verb's form tag. A
passive's participle carries no tense, so "was bitten" fell through to
`present`; the `was` carrier survived only as the `aux` label.

## Fix

Passive-branch tense now rides the be-carrier: `was` → past, `is` →
present (the `did` rule is unchanged and takes precedence). One rule,
expressed as `clauseTense`, replacing the inline expression.

## Limits

The same verb-form-only tense pattern exists in relative-clause and
other branches; no expectation covers them, so they are deliberately
untouched. Doubt raised during the fix and resolved: `was` is the only
past be-form; `are`/`be` are present and map through the `present`
default. Perfect passives ("has been bitten") were out of scope and
remain unmeasured.
