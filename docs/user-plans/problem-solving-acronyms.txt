--- First list (educators' acronyms for word problems involving
sequences) ---

C.U.B.E.S: Circle numbers, Underline question, Box key words,
Eliminate unnecessary info, Solve.
R.I.D.E.S: Read the problem, Identify what you need to find,
Determine the operation, Execute the plan, Check your work.
S.O.L.V.E: Study the problem, Organize the facts, Line up a plan,
Verify with action, Examine results.
T.I.P.S: Transform the problem, Identify the operation, Plan the
solution, Solve and check.
W.I.N.G.S: Write what you know, Identify the operation, Number
sentence, Graph or diagram, Solve and check.

--- Second list (math and general problem-solving education) ---

R.I.P.E.S.  Read the problem, Identify important info, Plan how to
solve, Execute the plan, Solve and check
G.U.E.S.S.  Given, Unknown, Equations, Substitute, Solve
S.T.A.R.    Search the problem, Translate into an equation, Answer,
Review
P.I.E.S.    Picture (draw a sketch), Information (circle key words),
Equation, Solve
R.U.N.S.    Read, Underline the question, Name the problem type,
Solve
K.N.O.W.S.  Know (what's given), Need to find, Organise, Work,
Solve/check
U.P.S. Check  Understand, Plan, Solve, Check
R.U.C.S.A.C.  Read, Understand, Choose the operation, Solve, Answer,
Check
R.E.A.D.E.R.  Read, Examine the questions, Abandon irrelevant info,
Determine the operation, Enter numbers, Record answer
I.D.E.A.L.  Identify, Define, Explore options, Action, Look back
R.I.D.E.    Remember the problem, Identify the operation, Do the
math, Evaluate the answer

User's notes on the second list: G.U.E.S.S. and S.T.A.R. lean more
toward algebraic/equation-based problems, while R.I.P.E.S., R.U.N.S.,
and K.N.O.W.S. are general-purpose word-problem frameworks.
I.D.E.A.L. is broader and used in both education and industry for
open-ended problem solving.
