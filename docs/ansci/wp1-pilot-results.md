# Slice 1 pilot — the 100-object classification run

Commissioned 2026-11-15. Sample: `wp1-pilot-sample.md` (frozen before
this run; classifications were deliberately not pre-filled). Protocol:
repair-programme §A.5 as ratified — aspects never the entity wholesale;
material / nature / function as separate assertions; function
phase-null; evidence A (attested) / B (attested correspondence,
B-derived via the H7 flavor bridge) / C (project-derived); composites
by components under H5; abstention a legitimate scored outcome.

**Method.** Pass 1: the primary classification below. Pass 2:
adjudication — every assertion re-checked against its stated basis;
rulings R1–R8 record where the protocol, not taste, decided.
Reproducibility: a second classifier re-did a stratified 20-object
subsample (every 5th object per domain) blind; the comparison is §6,
including its caveat. **Nothing in this document is banked into
`classify.ts`** — the pilot tests the procedure; banking its output
is a separate decision.

Notation: `Element · grade · confidence`; `—` = abstention on that
axis (a scored outcome, not a blank). Function is recorded in words
for all 100 objects and assigned an element for none, per protocol.

## 6 · Adjudication rulings (the procedure, made explicit)

Pass 2 re-checked every assertion against its stated basis. Eight
rulings did the work; they are the pilot's real product — a second
classifier given these rulings should land where this run landed.

- **R1 — Living plants are not materials.** The material axis asks
  for membership in a material class; a living plant is an organism,
  not a stuff. All 20 plants abstain on material; their products
  (timber, bamboo material, fibres) are classified where they appear
  as materials. The contrary reading （木 covers trees) was
  considered and rejected for this axis: it would collapse the
  bamboo ×3 domain distinction the sample was built to test.
- **R2 — Animal material = the five-livestock row only.** 雞/羊/牛/
  犬/豕 are attested at grade A. The creature-class schemes （五蟲）
  assign the other fifteen in sources that disagree with each
  other; a classification the sources can't stabilise is an
  abstention, not a coin-flip.
- **R3 — Stuff-class rule for substances and loose materials.**
  A substance's material axis is its stuff-class where a class
  exists (stone-class minerals → Earth · C; a metal → Metal;
  water itself → Water · A). Preparations, expressed juices, oils
  and animal products (silk, leather, horn, bone, beeswax) have no
  class anchor and abstain.
- **R4 — H5 as applied.** Single-material artefacts pass
  constitutive dominance by construction and take a whole-object
  assertion; composites get component assertions and a whole-object
  abstention; the working part (the lamp's flame, the abacus's
  beads) never governs.
- **R5 — The flavor bridge stays in its scope.** Every nature
  assertion above is the H7 shape: a recorded five-flavor value in a
  named bencao entry, composed with the attested flavor
  correspondence, scoped to the flavor aspect, graded B-derived —
  never B-direct, never a whole-entity classification. Multi-flavor
  records (rice wine, sheep, dog, snake) are recorded as recorded;
  no single element is forced. Thermal nature （寒熱溫涼） stayed
  phase-null throughout (the yin-yang axis, per the H7 adoption).
- **R6 — Animal nature at Benjing grade.** Livestock meat flavors
  and whole-animal entries （地龍， 白花蛇） are in; later-layer meat
  records (deer) and product records （殭蠶， 蟬蛻） are out, and
  the exclusions are stated in the rows, not silently omitted.
- **R7 — The glass family abstains.** Glass and ceramic glaze both
  abstain: the composition-vs-transformation conflict WP1b left
  open is not resolved by wanting a fuller table.
- **R8 — Cross-domain consistency.** The same stuff gets the same
  call in every domain it appears in: bamboo (material C · Wood;
  chopsticks C · Wood; flute C · Wood — confidences vary slightly
  with object context, element and grade do not), charcoal (C ·
  Wood · 0.50 in both rows), gold (A · Metal + 辛 B-derived in
  both rows), jade (C · Earth + 甘 B-derived in both rows).

**Adjudication outcome.** Of 95 classified axis-positions (49
material, 46 nature; component assertions counted within their
object's position), one assertion failed its stated basis under a
strict reading: ginger juice's nature — the Benjing flavor entry is
for the rhizome 生薑， and the juice is a preparation of it, so the
bridge's first condition (a flavor value recorded *for the
substance*) is met only by transfer. It is retained in the table
flagged by this paragraph and counted as unsupported in the scores.
Four further assertions survive only as flagged low-confidence
claims (well water's later-layer flavor, gourd's generic record,
carp's later-grade record, rope's type-dependent material) — their
flags are part of the assertions, so they stand as stated.

## 7 · Scores against the ratified bars

| Bar | Result | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Coverage ≥90% | axis coverage 95/200 = 47.5%; objects with ≥1 classified axis 80/100 = 80% | **NOT MET** under either plain reading |
| Accuracy ≥90% among classified | 94/95 ≈ 98.9% supported at their stated grades after adjudication | met |
| Per-domain accuracy floor 85% | lowest domain (Substances) 29/30 ≈ 96.7%; all others ≥98% | met |
| Unsupported assignments ≤5% | 1/95 ≈ 1.1% | met |
| Reproducibility (blind subsample) | 20/20 objects agree on element and grade on every axis — with the §8 caveat | met, qualified |

**The coverage miss is the pilot's headline finding, and it is a
finding about the bar, not about effort.** The sample was
deliberately loaded with cases the protocol *requires* abstention
on: 15 animals outside the attested livestock row, 6 animal-derived
or glass-family materials, 20 living plants on the material axis,
20 artefacts on the nature axis. A run that hit 90% axis coverage
could only do so by inventing anchors the tradition doesn't offer —
exactly what the protocol forbids. Either the ≥90% coverage bar was
intended for the scaled programme over a classifiable inventory
(not for a trap-loaded pilot sample), or its denominator needs
redefining (e.g. coverage of axes where an attested or derivable
anchor exists — on that denominator this run is 95/96 ≈ 99%, the
one miss being ash, where the anchor itself is contested). The
pilot does not choose between those readings; it hands the user
the numbers and the question, before the 500-object scale-up prices
the wrong one in.

The ratified amendment's own first-success criterion — *a
reproducible procedure, not full assignment* — is the bar this run
was actually shaped to meet, and on the evidence of §§6–8 it is
substantially met: the procedure is now eight explicit rulings, and
a second classifier following them reached the same calls.

## 8 · The blind subsample — and its caveat

The second classifier worked blind: protocol and object names only,
no access to the primary tables. Stratified subsample (every 5th
object per domain): chopsticks, stone mortar, bamboo flute, oil
lamp, honey, ash, lead oxide, limestone, raw cotton, gold, horn,
charcoal (material), chrysanthemum, rice plant, willow, reed, dog,
bee, swallow, spider.

**Result: agreement on all 20 objects, on every axis** — element,
grade, and abstention — including the trap resolutions (oil lamp
components-only; limestone's nature withheld because the record
belongs to calcined lime; honey's flavor not transferred to the
bee; the swallow's spring arrival refused as a property).
Confidences agreed exactly on most rows and within 0.1 on the rest
(bamboo flute 0.65 vs 0.70; oil lamp's vessel 0.60 vs 0.50).

**The caveat, stated plainly:** the blind run was genuinely blind,
but the primary classifier had *seen its output* before the primary
tables were finalised — the run returned while other work was in
flight. Agreement is therefore an upper bound, not an independent
reproduction, and this section must not be cited as one. Where the
blind run's reasoning was simply the protocol read correctly (R1
above all), the primary adopted it on its merits and the ruling
records why. For the 500-object run, the ordering discipline is
the fix, and it is the C.5 discipline applied to ourselves: the
primary classifications are committed to file *before* the blind
output is opened.

## 9 · What this does not do

Nothing here is banked into `classify.ts`. The pilot's outputs are
candidate assertions with provenance, held in this document; the
H7-shaped nature assertions in particular were produced under the
pilot's remit as a classification procedure and are not thereby
admitted to the graph — banking any of this is a separate,
separately approved pass. The 500-object scale-up waits on the
user's ruling on the coverage denominator in §7.
