## What was built

**The obligations register (social.ts).** The Conversation
register gains `obligations`: the demand ids the reading has
raised and the Planner has not yet discharged. `readSocial` now
records each raised demand there as well as annotating it. The
unit stays the registers' one writer: the Planner discharges
through the unit's `dischargeDemand`, and asks what it needs
through `conversationPhase` / `openDemands` (design §3.2's seam —
the interface has no answer about content, wording or knowledge
to give).

**The farewell move (turn.ts).** A leave-taking object is now
answered the way a greeting is: the Planner authors a reply
object from the object's own graph and the Verbalizer generates
the mirror — the register the person chose is the register
returned ("Goodbye." → "Goodbye.", "Bye." → "Bye.", "Farewell."
→ "Farewell."). The reply object carries streamRole "closes"
and ritual standing (reply.ts widened for the ritual kinds).
Discharging farewell-return completes the closing in the same
turn (closing → closed in `dischargeDemand`), replacing S1's
next-turn presumption for the normal path; the presumption
remains for any path where a closing survives a turn boundary.
Before S2, a leave-taking fell through to the statement
steering and was answered "Ok. Noted." — the free-conversation
probe's finding, closed at its location.

**The thanks receipt (turn.ts, plan.ts).** A thanks object is
answered with the receipt move: a bare predication — "welcome"
predicated of the person — authored by the Planner as
`planThanksReceipt` and generated by the Verbalizer ("You are
welcome."). No wording is looked up. Thanks stays ritual: the
pending thread survives it untouched and the turn ends there,
like the other ritual exchanges.

**Phase consulted (turn.ts).** `applyInitiative` now returns
early when the Conversation phase is closing or closed (design
§6: no initiative in closing/closed). A farewell turn's own
reply passes through that function, so without the guard an
initiative could append itself to a goodbye; a test stages
exactly that (the R6-1 unexpressed-state setup with a farewell
as the eligible turn) and pins that none fires.

**Reopening made general (social.ts).** S1 read an arrival in a
closed conversation as a fresh opening only for greetings. The
design's machine says anything arriving in closed reopens as a
new opening; S2 makes it general, because the phase is now
consulted — a conversation stuck at closed would otherwise bar
initiative for the rest of the session. A statement after
goodbye is read as a fresh opening, annotated as such.

**The greeting branch** additionally records the discharge of
greeting-return (its behaviour — the mirror — is unchanged from
before the unit existed). Self-answer obligations are recorded
and stand open: S3 discharges them, and a test pins that they
stand.

## A located gap found on the way (not S2's to fix)

**Probe-poisoning in the sentence layer's WordNet provider.**
Parsing "You are welcome." twice in one process gives two
different parses: the first reads "welcome" as a predicate
quality and generates "You are welcome."; the second reads it
as a verb and generates "You welcome." Minimal reproduction,
fresh process: parse the string, parse it again. The mechanism:
`wordnetVerb` (wordnet-lex.ts) registers the verb entry it
derives into the shared runtime lexicon as a side effect, and
parse.ts probes `wordnetVerb` while scanning — so a mere probe
during the first parse permanently re-lexicalises the word for
every later parse in the process. Any word with both verb and
adjective/noun derivations is exposed. The receipt therefore
authors its graph by construction rather than by parsing a
canonical string (the reason is recorded at `planThanksReceipt`).
The bug itself is a sentence-layer fix, outside this slice; it
is recorded here so it is not lost. Nothing in S2's gates
depends on the poisoned route.

## What this does not show

The moves are proven against hand-run conversations through the
real turn loop, not against the sealed probe set — that run is
the holder's to run when the owner commissions it, at the end
of the S2–S5 sequence. Nobody independent has checked the
receipt's wording choice ("You are welcome.") against the bar;
it is the canonical receipt, authored as a predication, and the
design words no move — the Verbalizer's output from the move's
graph is the behaviour. The probe-poisoning gap above is
diagnosed with a minimal reproduction but not fixed; words
beyond "welcome" may be affected in long conversations in ways
no gate here measures.
