# Slice 1 scale-up — the 400-object test sample for the 500 run

Prepared 2026-11-15 under Part E §E.5. The pilot's 100 objects
(`wp1-pilot-sample.md`, classified in `wp1-pilot-results.md`) are
the run's **calibration subset**; this sheet is the **test
subset**: 400 fresh objects, 80 per domain, same discipline as
the pilot sample — plain cases, composites, and traps, with
cross-domain repeats on purpose. No classifications are filled
in; pre-filling would contaminate the test subset exactly as it
would have the pilot.

**The run this feeds** is scored on Part E's three evaluations —
heuristic coverage (per tier), validated accuracy, abstention
discipline — under the five-tier ladder (T1 attested · T2
established correspondence · T3 registered-heuristic inference ·
T4 novel prediction · T5 unresolved). The rules in force are the
pilot's R1–R8 as split by Part E §E.6, H5/H6/H7, and
P2 — CREATURE_CLASS_YUELING_V1, whose class predictions this
sample's animal domain is built to exercise. Sealed ordering
applies (Part E §E.5): primary classifications are committed to
file before any blind output is opened.

## Traps built in on purpose

- **P2 exercise set:** the animal domain is heavy with clear
  class members (birds, fish, furred mammals, shelled water
  creatures) and with the commentarial boundary cases Part E
  flags (tiger, second occurrence — Zheng Xuan's 倮 placement
  is T4, not text).
- **Second occurrences:** some forty pilot objects recur, marked
  as such. They are the run's internal consistency check —
  R8 (same stuff, same call, same tier) is scored on them
  mechanically before any judging.
- **Composites and working parts:** oil press, tofu mould,
  bird cage, fish trap — H5 discipline, components first.
- **Substance vs phenomenon, again:** collected rainwater
  recurs as a substance; nothing in this sheet licenses a
  whole-phenomenon weather classification (H6).
- **Flavor-keyed items:** sichuan pepper, star anise, turmeric,
  mustard, lemon juice — H7's bridge, flavor aspect only.
