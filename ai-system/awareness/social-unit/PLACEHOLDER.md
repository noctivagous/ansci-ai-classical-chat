# Social Unit

Schematic address: `awareness/social-unit` · Type: Unit · State: partial · 95% completed to goal

Awareness of the interlocutor as a social presence, not only as a source of statements. Holds the Interlocutor and Conversation. Designed 2026-11-18 (design: `~/workspace/ansci-social-unit-design.md`; placement inside Awareness confirmed at design time, owner's rulings 1a–5a).

Slice S1 (2026-11-18): the social reading runs in the turn loop after Coherence's inbound analysis and annotates each assessed object in shadow — no reply changes — while the Interlocutor and Conversation registers live in the Session and update from the reading. Assessment recognises leave-taking, thanks, and self-query objects beside the greeting.

Slice S2 (2026-11-18, under the owner's sequence authorization for S2–S5): the first demands are live. The reading records each raised demand in the Conversation register's obligations; the Planner discharges greeting-return (the greeting mirror, as before), farewell-return (a new mirror branch — the register the person chose is the register returned, as a closing reply object) and thanks-receipt (a bare predication authored in plan.ts) as it authors those moves, and the unit records each discharge — discharging the farewell completes the closing in the same turn. The phase is consulted before initiative (no initiative in closing or closed), and an arrival in a closed conversation reopens it as a fresh opening for any act kind, not only greetings. The greeting realisation variants (to-the-point / welcoming) remain the owner's configurations ruling, still open; the mirror is the behaviour. Remaining: self-facts and self-answers (S3), clarification (S4), the premise stance (S5); cross-conversation persistence (S6) is deferred by ruling 5a until a deployment has identified returners.

Slice S3 (2026-11-18, same authorization): self-facts as data in Awareness Self's tables (`src/ansci/identity.ts`, `src/ansci/capabilities.ts`), requested by the unit — never copied into it — and self-queries answered from that record: the identity answer predicates the name from the Identity table as a reply object with the table's kindLine beside it; the capability answer presents the Capabilities & limits table as stored. The capability shape needed the sentence layer to draw it first: parse.ts gained the lexical-do pass ("What can you do?" failed with "no verb found" — every do-form was classified as an auxiliary), and assess.ts recognises the shape (verb root "do", agent "you", modal "can", questioned "what"). Remaining: clarification (S4), the premise stance (S5); S6 deferred by ruling 5a.

Slice S4 (2026-11-18, same authorization): the clarification move end to end. The Conversation pending register is live (opened, read, counted and closed only through social.ts's functions); the turn reads a standing clarification against the next turn first — a fill naming an offered candidate resolves the point (a token fill's word is substituted into the original sentence, which is then processed; a sense fill settles the lemma's reading for the conversation), a usable new turn abandons the pending, and a turn that is neither counts as an attempt: re-asked once, then the system proceeds on its best reading and says so. All four trigger classes are wired at their located sources: (a) the parse's token record (weak or unresolved resolutions, genuine rivals only — a case-only candidate names no choice — with the offered candidates filtered by the resolver's own parse-retry test; no candidates → the rephrase request), (b) the R3 rival-split site, which now also opens the pending and respects clarified readings, (c) the unanchored-definite site (the context request), (d) the parse-failure return, whose reply is now the rephrase request that names no failure. Remaining: the premise stance (S5); S6 deferred by ruling 5a.

Slice S5 (2026-11-18, same authorization): the premise stance. Coherence supplies the degree (`premiseDegree` in coherence.ts: contradiction by the record — a standing state in the asked quality's slot with another value — or by the graph — a path to the aspect's other value over the question-answerer's own edge family; anything else is absence, §7c's territory). The unit owns the response pattern: the stance's three beats at two sites (the record answer completed with its re-offer; the graph decline replaced by the stance), the stance-repair pending, and the insistence handling — a repeated why-question is answered under the supposition framing, an asserted premise is not recorded and not adopted, and either way the disagreement is recorded in the Conversation register. The question's own recording of a presupposed quality is set aside at the stance site (T-R7.5's discipline), so the false premise never enters the record as a state. Slices S1–S5 are built; S6 (cross-conversation persistence) remains deferred under ruling 5a — no deployment has identified returners, so its gate cannot yet be exercised.

Slice S6 (2026-11-18, the owner's commission, ahead of ruling 5a's deployment precondition, on synthetic-returner gates): Interlocutor persistence across conversations, identity-keyed. The store (`src/ansci/interlocutor-store.ts` — memory and JSON-file implementations) holds the durable core only: the stated name, the conversation count, and the established topics as renderings (within a conversation the register holds pointers, never copies, and pointers cannot cross conversations — the topics' names are the persisted form). The store is consulted at exactly one moment — when the interlocutor states their identity in the present conversation; a record makes the contact "returning", loads the prior picture into the register, and the naming turn's reply acknowledges the return ("Welcome back, Sam."). Before that statement no recognition is performed; a visitor who never names themselves writes nothing and is first contact every time. The durable core is rewritten at every turn's end once an identity is known, so a conversation ending without a farewell has still left its core. The chat demonstrator wires the file store (path is deployment configuration, `ANSCI_INTERLOCUTOR_STORE`). With S6, all six slices of the design are built; the programme's remaining step is the sealed probe acceptance run, the holder's to commission.
