# Coherence Analysis

Schematic address: `coherence/coherence-analysis` · Type: Unit · State: placeholder · 0% completed to goal

Works in both directions, at several levels. Inbound: how what comes in makes sense — the words and the sentence, the situation, the stream of what has been said; what doesn't fit, and what information is missing (e.g. an unknown person's 'I was furious' → 'Furious about what?'). Outbound: used by the Verbalizer — what goes out is checked as it is verbalized, so that what is said makes sense too.
