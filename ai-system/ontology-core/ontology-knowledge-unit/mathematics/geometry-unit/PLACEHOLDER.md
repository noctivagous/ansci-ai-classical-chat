# Geometry Unit

- **Schematic address:** `ontology-core/ontology-knowledge-unit/mathematics/geometry-unit` — inside Mathematics in the Ontology Knowledge Unit
- **Type:** Unit
- **State:** placeholder — **0% completed to goal** (set by us, assessed by the project, not measured or computed). Placeholder seat placed 2026-11-19. Nothing is designed and nothing is built.

## What stands here

A seat, and the ruling that put it here — nothing more. The owner
directed (2026-11-18, ProblemSolving design §9) that the Geometry
Unit stand inside **Mathematics**, in the same larger unit
container as the Quantification Unit, and that the two will work
with each other. The placement was ruled the same day and the seat
placed 2026-11-19.

The unit's contents have never been stated: no scope, no data, no
machinery, no design. What geometry the system touches today
lives elsewhere — the UnitsMeasurement Unit's area dimension
(inside the QuantificationUnit, beside this seat) is the one place
quantity and geometry already meet, and the Senses-Scening Unit's
scene placements carry spatial relations in the language layer.
Neither is this unit, and neither was moved.

## What is deliberately not here yet

- **No Geometry need kind.** Per the 2026-11-18 ruling, a geometry
  need kind is registered in ProblemSolving's need registry when
  this unit is built (its name is settled at that build;
  "geometry" is the plain candidate). Until then, geometry-shaped
  problems route nowhere and are not forced under
  "quantification".
- **No MathPS delegation target.** ProblemSolving's MathPS
  section will delegate geometry solving here when the unit
  exists; today there is nothing to delegate to, and the record
  says so rather than standing a façade.
