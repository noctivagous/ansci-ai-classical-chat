- **Schematic address:** `ontology-core`

## What this container is

The owner's direction (2026-11-16): "the EDU will sit inside the
OntologyCore on the map."

The OntologyCore is the system's ontological heart — the container for
what the world is made of and how its kinds correspond, in the system's
own terms: the Five Phases and their cycles, the correspondence framework,
and the authorities that speak for parts of it.

## Placed inside so far

- **Ontology Knowledge Unit (OKU)** — `ontology-core/ontology-knowledge-unit`
  — contains the correspondence graph and the classification table, moved
  here from Knowledge at the owner's direction (2026-11-16).
- **EmotionDesireUnit (EDU)** — `ontology-core/emotion-desire-unit` — the
  medium-neutral authority on emotion and desire （七情六欲）, reached by
  each medium's bridge unit (the LPU's LEDU today; a future IDU for the
  Image Processing Unit).
