## What this is

The **ErrorFailure Unit** (owner's direction, 2026-11-20): the unit
that **authors the account of why something could not be done**.

When a problem-solving walk exhausts its strategies or reaches its
loop budget, or a route ends with a need undischarged, the record of
the attempt comes here — the problem, the strategies tried, the loops
spent, the step where it stopped, the capability or information
lacked, and which holdings were checked before the lack was declared —
and this unit composes the explanation from that record. **It
assembles; it does not invent.** An account that names anything the
record does not hold is a defect of this unit, not a style.

Its working counterpart inside Reasoning is the ProblemSolving Unit's
**ReasoningErrorFailure Unit**, which assembles attempt records where
the solving happens and files them here — the system's bridge pattern:
work at the site, authority at the home.

Modules that need failure accounts get their own counterpart
subunit that works with this unit — the ReasoningErrorFailure Unit
inside ProblemSolving is the first — **so they use this unit's
generic functionality instead of reproducing it**.

## Standing rules

- **It never speaks.** Its accounts are typed records; the Planner
  authors any reply that carries one, as with every unit's output.
- **Accounts accumulate.** The filed accounts are the system's failure
  knowledge: the same lack recurring is a located work item — a
  capability to build, knowledge to bank, a strategy to repair — not
  a repeated surprise.
- **A priced, located failure is a legitimate outcome.** Reaching a
  loop budget or exhausting the registered strategies ends a problem
  in an account, not in silence and not in invention.

## What is deliberately not here yet

- **No account machinery.** No record type, no filing route, no
  accumulation store is designed or built.
- **No producer wired.** Neither the ProblemSolving walk (itself
  designed, not built) nor the turn's decline path files anything
  here yet.
- **Nothing is built.** This file and the map entry are the whole of
  it: a named seat, awaiting its design slice.
